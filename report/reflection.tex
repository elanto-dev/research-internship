\chapter{Reflection on research process}\label{reflection}
\section{Research results}
In conclusion, the chosen methodologies were effective in producing interpretable results from the collected developer-\acrshort{gpt} conversations. All approaches returned core lexican and sequential patterns found in the data, and the similarity of results across methods strengthened the confidence in their validity. Although the applied methods rely heavily on frequent words and phrases, the use of multiple methods enabled verification of findings from different angles.

While more advanced approaches such as BERTopic or other transformer-based methods could have found more subtle or abstract patterns, these were not applied due to limited knowledge in deep learning. Instead, I prioritized transparency and control through well-understood, classical techniques. Future work may benefit from incorporating neural models to explore more subtle and less frequent interaction patterns.

\section{Process}
The research internship began in February 2024 with the selection of the topic in collaboration with the supervisor. The research methods were defined early in the process, followed by the implementation phase, which occupied the first three months. During this period, the main components were developed and refined, and the initial drafting of the accompanying research article was initiated. After this initial phase, the project went through an extended pause until April 2025. When the project restarted, the focus was on finalizing the implementation and completing the research article.

\section{Problems encountered}
There were no significant technical or methodological difficulties encountered during the research process. The selected approaches allowed the generation of coherent and interpretable results. Both the implementation of analytical methods and the interpretation and documentation of findings did not cause any issues. 

However, the primary limitation affecting the progress of the research was external: personal life circumstances, followed by a master's thesis, led to a need to pause the internship until the thesis was done. As a result, the overall timeline of the project was delayed. This pause impacted momentum and reduced the originality of the research, as several new publications released during that period that addressed related themes in developer–chatbot interaction and the role of \acrshort{gpt} in these interactions.
