\chapter{Introduction}\label{introduction}

\Acrfull{llm} is a statistical model that is trained on the large amount of data containing billions of parameters and that uses previous tokens to predict future tokens~\cite{gpt-usage-and-lim}. \Acrshort{llm}s are used in different domains such as conversation agents, education, text summarization, explainable AI, information retrieval and many more~\cite{Dale_2021}. One of the most well-known models is \acrfull{gpt}~\cite{Floridi2020-gpt3paper} --- a chatbot developed by OpenAI and first released in November 2022. According to OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, \acrshort{gpt} reached 100 million weekly active users in November 2023\footnote{\url{https://www.theverge.com/2023/11/6/23948619/openai-chatgpt-devday-developer-conference-news}}. 

\Acrshort{gpt} drew attention for its detailed and expressive answers across many domains of knowledge, including software development. The chatbot was adopted by software engineers as an alternative to using Google for solving software development-related issues. Studies have investigated \acrshort{gpt}'s efficacy when used in software development processes~\cite{Hörnemalm_2023}, its limitations~\cite{gpt-usage-and-lim}, domains of application within issue-tracking systems, reliance on the \acrshort{gpt}-generated code~\cite{gpt-issue-tracking} and the correctness of the generated code~\cite{gpt-code-correctness, zhang2023critical}. Additionally, some studies focus on human-bot interaction: the role of \acrshort{gpt} in the process of collaborative software architecting~\cite{gpt-software-architecture}, or what \acrshort{gpt}'s primary use is in issue-tracking systems~\cite{gpt-issue-tracking}. 

In this study we focus on the domain of human-bot interaction and use developer prompts to extract the types of collaboration that developers want \acrshort{gpt} to follow during their interaction. There are many ways developers can collaborate with \acrshort{gpt}, and we focus on extracting patterns from their prompts to recognize what type of collaboration the developer expects from \acrshort{gpt}. This leads to the research question of this study: \textbf{What role does \acrshort{gpt} take in user-chatbot interaction within the software development domain?}

To answer the research question, we used various supervised and unsupervised text mining techniques: n-grams extraction, \acrfull{spm} and topic modeling. These methods help us with understanding the common themes and topics present in developers' \acrshort{gpt} prompts and the expectations they place on \acrshort{gpt} in these prompts. Topic modeling provides information that describes the data, while \acrshort{spm} and n-grams are used to provide the initial overview of what are the most common words/word combinations present in the data. We apply the mentioned techniques on DevGPT~\cite{devgpt} and its extension datasets that consist of developer-\acrshort{gpt} conversations sourced from GitHub\footnote{\url{https://github.com}} commits, pull requests, code files, issues and discussion, and from HackerNews\footnote{\url{https://news.ycombinator.com}}. 

The answer to this research question provides valuable insights into the role that \acrshort{gpt} plays in developer-chatbot collaboration. Additionally, it shows what tasks developers trust chatbots like \acrshort{gpt} to perform and what to expect from \acrshort{gpt} in that role. By outlining the patterns of developer-chatbot interaction, we provide the ground for new research to develop or enhance chatbots' ability to help the software developer in the way that is most expected from the chatbot.

We firstly provide some background information and related research in Chapter~\ref{related-work}. Then we describe in detail what the dataset consists of and what pre-processing steps are taken in Chapter~\ref{sec:data}. Chapter~\ref{methodology} focuses on methods used to explore the data. Chapter~\ref{results} contains the results of the research and their analysis. Chapter~\ref{conclusions} concludes the research by discussing the methods and results of the study, and establishing the future research.
